# BATCH 01 — REDDIT HORROR STORIES

## Objective
Measure which first-5-second hook pacing style produces the highest percentage of UPLOADABLE outputs.

## Locked Parameters
* Duration: 40s
* Subtitle preset: Fixed (Large kinetic captions)
* Gameplay style: Fixed (GTA + dark/night clips)
* Voice profile: Fixed (Medium-fast male narration)
* Music style: Fixed (Low dark ambient)

## Variable Under Test
ONLY: **Hook pacing in first 5 seconds**

*Variants to Test:*
1. Immediate shock opening
2. Delayed reveal at 2s
3. Fast narration burst
4. Question-based hook
5. Whisper/slow suspense opening
6. "True story" opening
7. Loud emotional conflict opening
8. Countdown/tension opening
9. Curiosity gap opening
10. Visual-first silent opening

## Success Metrics
* Viewer attention in first 3 seconds
* Creator willingness to upload immediately
* Subtitle readability
* Narration emotional intensity
* Render stability

## Hard Failure Conditions
Immediately classify DEAD if:
* Hook feels slow
* Subtitle overlap occurs
* Audio energy collapses
* Gameplay repetition becomes obvious
* Viewer would likely scroll before 5 seconds

## Batch Review Process
For each render:
1. Watch first 5 seconds only
2. Score emotional pull
3. Decide: UPLOADABLE, FIXABLE, or DEAD
4. Move files accordingly

## Golden Rule
Do not attempt perfection. Optimize only for:
"Idea → usable downloadable short-form video with minimal friction."
