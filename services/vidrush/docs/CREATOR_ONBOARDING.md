# VidRush Alpha - Creator Onboarding

Welcome to the VidRush Alpha! You are one of the first creators to test the Autonomous AI Media Operating System.

## How to Generate a Video
1. Enter your Topic.
2. Select your Style (Reddit Story, Horror Short, or Motivational Edit).
3. Click **Generate**.
4. Wait for the Preview, then click **Export**.

## Expected Performance
- **Max Duration:** 3 Minutes
- **Expected Render Time:** ~2-4 minutes depending on queue length.

## Known Limitations
- Heavy customizations are disabled during Alpha.
- Occasional subtitle timing drift might occur.

## How to Report Issues
If a render fails or the hook is weak, click the **Thumbs Down / Report Issue** button on the dashboard. Every report directly trains our system. Thank you for building with us!
